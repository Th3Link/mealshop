# Shops and optional learning

Shop filtering and shopping sessions belong in the shopping view. Shop management and ingredient first/
second choice preferences are explicit. Move an unavailable item to its fallback without changing any
quantity/source. Shop-specific up/down controls provide a keyboard/touch alternative to dragging.

Learning is disabled by default. Starting an opted-in session names the shop and says that check-off
order is recorded locally for this household. Only completed sessions count; unchecks/corrections
invalidate observations. At least three valid sessions per item are needed before a learned position
is applied. Show manual/default/uncertain/learned status and sample counts. Manual ordering wins.

History can be inspected, reset and disabled independently of shopping data. No location access,
cross-household data, dietary inference or remote model. Reflect loading/failure/conflict states.
