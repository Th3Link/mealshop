# Demo localization UX

- A visible language selector offers Deutsch and English. Initial preference: saved choice,
  otherwise the browser language (de variants map to German, unsupported languages to English).
- All demo controls/statuses have keyed messages. German ingredient names are shown for reviewed
  starter record identities; custom/unknown records use their original names without guessed translations.
- Search matches localized labels, canonical names and aliases in both supported languages.
- German gram input uses a comma, English a dot; no grouping separators are accepted in inputs.
  Outputs may group digits. Switching language preserves exact values and results; invalid
  fields keep their text and require correction rather than being reinterpreted in the new locale.
- Original-language provenance, uncertainty and education are labelled explicitly. Missing
  translations must not hide limitations or imply content has been reviewed in German.
- Header/title/footer say Demo; replace marketing copy with a pragmatic explanation. No redesign.
