# Hosted plans, paid app and recipe marketplace

Status: Draft
Priority: Could
Origin: Customer
Milestone: M4

## User value
Sustain the project with hosted tiers, paid app distribution and paid content.

## Acceptance criteria
1. Given hosted registration, free and paid tiers describe their entitlements; prices/limits remain explicit product decisions rather than invented defaults.
2. Given the official hosted service, paid signed distribution is intended as an admission condition with server-side entitlement checks; an embedded app secret alone is insufficient.
3. Given a self-compiled client and an own server, AGPL rights and access to self-hosted features remain intact.
4. Given a paid recipe or collection, purchase, author entitlement, licence, delivery, refunds and imported-content exclusion are validated before charging anyone.
5. Given source distribution, complete corresponding source and applicable AGPL notices remain available independently of app purchase.

## Out of scope
Payments or production app attestation in this bootstrap.

## UX notes
UI flows, accessibility, empty/error states and user review points must be specified before Ready.
