# UX review

Nextcloud 35 and official components remain the design authority. UI UX Pro Max's error-recovery,
visible-label, state-preservation and form-feedback guidance applies; Taste contributes aligned edges,
one visual system, legible hierarchy and screenshot review. Its marketing heroes, palettes, font kits,
motion effects and static landing-page gates do not apply to this authenticated embedded application.

Observed before implementation: household profiles show all fourteen allergens vertically followed by
three large text areas; sorbitol appears as both a canonical token and a checkbox. Existing meals are
headed “Plan a meal”; cancel silently removes edits. Catalogue empty results only clear search even
when favourites hide everything. Recipe lists have no search. Failed recipe/profile mutations expose
an unconditional reload. Profile/meal household switches lack the recipe editor's route-update guard.

Use progressive disclosure for supplemental notes, keeping hard exclusions visible. Use explicit
discard confirmation only for changed drafts. Keep one primary action per form, announce saves
without moving focus, and focus newly revealed reviews. Do not replace Nextcloud navigation, icons,
colours or focus styles. Review before/after screenshots at desktop and small phone widths.
