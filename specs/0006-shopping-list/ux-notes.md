# Native shopping experience

One household shopping destination, with recipe/meal-plan contributions and manually entered goods.
In shopping mode, a large named checkbox completes the remaining contributions of an item; purchased
items move to a separate disclosure and can be unchecked. Partial purchases remain visible. Show exact
base quantities (g, ml, pieces), with original input/package units in contributor details.

Recipes are pinned by revision and servings. Users explicitly add/update/remove a recipe source, or
review selected meal slots before synchronizing them. Updating/removing sources changes only outstanding
requirements: purchased receipts retain their original recipe/package provenance. Manual contributions
are independent. No implicit stock reallocation or ml-to-g conversion.

Use official Nextcloud controls, visible labels, predictable focus, pending-action locks, concise success
and recoverable conflict feedback. Include accessible empty/read-only/error/loading states, de/en keyed
messages, native themes and responsive layouts. Never run browser tests against customer households.
