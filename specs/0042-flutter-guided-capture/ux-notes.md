# UX notes

Mirror the Nextcloud entry: a title, one calm sentence ("Take what is nearest. You look over it
once before anything is saved.") and four large cards — Paste or dictate, Photo or screenshot,
From a website, Start empty. One column on phones.

- Paste/dictate: a large text field with a microphone button, live preview of title, servings,
  ingredients and steps below; unresolved lines are marked, not hidden.
- Photo: camera or gallery, rotate, then the picture stays pinned above the text while it is
  corrected. Printed/handwritten is detected or chosen with one toggle. Recognition shows progress
  and can be cancelled.
- Review is one shared screen for all ways, ending in the existing editor contract.
- A missing model explains its size and purpose before any download; refusing keeps typing.
- No confidence percentages presented as accuracy. Check DE/EN, large text, `TalkBack`, offline.
