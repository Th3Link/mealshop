# UX notes

- **Say it, then confirm.** The first control on the page is one sentence field with a
  microphone. The answer repeats what was understood, with the real date, in one line.
- **Never act on a guess.** Ambiguous titles become buttons; unknown ones are quoted back. The
  plan changes only through the reviewed apply.
- **Keep the consequence in view.** The planner is a panel on the trailing edge (full width on
  phones), not a form below the fold. Focus moves to the review heading when a preview arrives.
- **Quiet week.** Empty slots are a single line with a plus; planned meals are cards with an
  accent edge; today is marked in words and colour.
- **Human units.** Durations read "1 h 10 min"; the exact seconds stay in the tooltip.

Nextcloud components and theme variables only. Targets are at least 44 px. The command answer is
a polite live region. Known rough edges kept for later: the planner still lists preparation,
eating days, time limits and calendar snapshot in one column, and the review is long.
